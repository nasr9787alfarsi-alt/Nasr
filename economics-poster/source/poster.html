Who’s Cooking the Economy?

Scarce ingredients, one big question: who decides what to cook, how, and for whom?

BSEC1103 · Principles of EconomicsChapter 2 · Economic Systems

1What is an economic system?

The means by which a society organises and distributes its resources, goods and services: its mode of production and distribution. (Fernando, 2026)

2Why every society needs one

Resources are scarce, wants are unlimited, so every society must choose. Its system answers: (Mankiw, 2021)

WHAT?
HOW?
FOR WHOM?
