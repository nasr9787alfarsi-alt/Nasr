1Meaning

An economic system is the means by which a society or government organises and distributes its resources, goods and services: its mode of production and distribution. (Fernando, 2026)

2Why societies need one

Resources are scarce but wants are unlimited, so every society must make choices. Its economic system answers the three central questions → (Mankiw, 2021)

4Oman: a mixed economy

Public & private sectors co-exist under national planning.

Government roles

  • Plans the economy: Vision 2040
  • Owns state companies: Oman Investment Authority
  • Protects consumers & workers by law

Private sector roles

  • Leads diversification away from oil
  • Partners the state in PPP projects

(Oman Vision 2040 Main Committee, 2019; Royal Decree No. 66/2014, 52/2019, 61/2020, 53/2023)
