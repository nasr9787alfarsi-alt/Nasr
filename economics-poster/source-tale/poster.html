Capitalist Economymarket · free
  • Private ownership; no government interference
  • Freedom to start any business
  • Consumers buy by income; firms offer variety
  • Guided by profit & competition
Mixed Economypublic + private
  • Public & private sectors co-exist
  • Both decide output & distribution
  • Economic planning controls the economy
  • Protects consumer & labour rights
Socialist Economycommand
  • Government owns & controls resources
  • Central authority decides everything
  • Fixed, reasonable prices; quality goods
  • Aim: social welfare & equal jobs for all

THE SEQUEL: OMAN

Oman: a mixed economy

GOVERNMENT SECTOR

Plans the economy: Oman Vision 2040
Owns state firms: Oman Investment Authority
Protects consumers & workers by law

PRIVATE SECTOR

Leads diversification beyond oil
Partners the state in PPP projects
Takes over state firms via privatisation
(Oman Vision 2040 Main Committee, 2019; Royal Decrees 66/2014, 51/2019, 52/2019, 61/2020, 53/2023)
EPILOGUENo system is perfect; the mixed economy blends market freedom with social welfare, the model Oman follows.
