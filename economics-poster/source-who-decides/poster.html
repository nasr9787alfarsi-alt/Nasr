What is an economic system?

The means by which a society or government organises and distributes its resources, goods and services: its mode of production and distribution. (Fernando, 2026)

Why does every society need one?

Resources are scarce but wants are unlimited, so a society needs a system to answer the three central questions: what, how and for whom to produce. (Mankiw, 2021)

THREE ECONOMIC SYSTEMS
Capitalist Economymarket · free
Core functioning

Resources privately owned by citizens & institutions; no government interference.

  • Freedom to start any business
  • Consumers buy by income; firms offer variety
  • Guided by profit and competition
Flowchart
Socialist Economycommand
Core functioning

Government owns & controls production; a central authority decides all.

  • State owns banks, industry, transport
  • Prices fixed; quality goods, reasonable prices
  • Aim: social welfare & equal jobs for all
Flowchart
Mixed Economypublic + private
Core functioning

Private + government control: both decide what is made & how it is shared.

  • Public & private sectors co-exist
  • Planning controls the economy
  • Profit + social interest; consumer & labour rights
