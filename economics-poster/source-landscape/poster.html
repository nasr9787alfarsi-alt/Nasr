Who Pulls the Strings?

Every society must decide what, how and for whom to produce. An economic system is the answer to: who decides?

BSEC1103 · Principles of EconomicsChapter 2 · Economic Systems
ACT I · THE SCRIPT

What is an economic system?

The means by which a society or government organises and distributes its resources, goods and services: its mode of production and distribution. (Fernando, 2026)

Why does a society need one?

Resources are scarce but wants are unlimited, so every society must choose. Its system answers 3 central questions: (Mankiw, 2021)

WHAT?
HOW?
FOR WHOM?
Capitalist Economymarket · free
  • Resources privately owned
  • Freedom to start any business
  • Buyers choose, by their income
  • Guided by profit & competition
Mixed Economypublic + private
  • Public & private sectors co-exist
  • Both decide output & distribution
  • Planning controls the economy
  • Protects consumer & labour rights
Socialist Economycommand
  • State owns banks & industry
  • Central authority decides all
  • Fixed, reasonable prices
  • Equal jobs for all; social welfare

ACT IV · THE FINALE

Oman’s stage: a mixed economy

Government sector

Plans the economy: Oman Vision 2040
Owns state firms: Oman Investment Authority
Protects consumers & workers by law

Private sector

Leads diversification beyond oil
Partners the state in PPP projects
Takes over state firms via privatisation
(Oman Vision 2040 Main Committee, 2019; Royal Decrees 66/2014, 51/2019, 52/2019, 61/2020, 53/2023)
