# ALEXANDRIA: 40-second trailer (39.8 s)

This is a separate final version. The full film and the 1:14 trailers are unchanged. It has 25
shots, and the grade, typography and title design are the same as the full film. Nothing is sped
up.

## Music: the strongest 40 seconds

- **Choice of section.** The song's most powerful music is its climax, 285–293 s (−14.4 dB, the
  loudest block in the song), and its natural ending follows straight after. The film is built on
  that final stretch.
- **Opening (0–3.5 s).** The intro's hush (song 9.85–13.38 s) plays under the traveller and the
  approaching headlight.
- **The cut.** Where the band would drop in, the edit cuts to the accent at 276.36 s, the biggest
  hit in the second half of the song. The match score is 0.913, against a 0.875 median for random
  pairs, and the whole song stays in A. There is a 30 ms crossfade and no click.
- **The rest.** The song then plays untouched through the peak and its own ending. The last note
  fades out over the end card.
- **Loudness.** −13.9 LUFS, with a −1.2 dB peak.
