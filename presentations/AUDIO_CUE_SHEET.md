# Sound cue sheet — *The Breaker That Kept Tripping* (executive edition)

**Principle:** sound is a precision tool, not entertainment. There is no music, no background audio and no soundtrack. Sound plays only when something important happens on screen.

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `ZAZ-8_Breaker_That_Kept_Tripping_Executive_AUDIO.pptx` | Main file with event-driven sound effects (≈2.4 MB) |
| `ZAZ-8_Breaker_That_Kept_Tripping_Executive_SILENT.pptx` | **Silent backup**: same slides, animations and notes, no audio (≈1.8 MB) |
| `ZAZ-8_audio_preview.mp3` | All sound cues in presentation order, 1.5 s apart (≈45 s), to audition before the meeting |

## At a glance

* **12 of 31 slides** have sound. **19 are completely silent.**
* **16 short sounds** in total. The longest has about 2.9 s of audible sound (the root-cause reveal); most last under 1 s.
* **No overlaps:** checked automatically for every click. Sound sequences (stamps, the before/after pulse) are pre-mixed into single files, so nothing cuts anything else off.
* **Nothing carries over:** every slide without its own sound starts with a "stop sound" instruction. Sounds attached to animations also stop when you click forward.
* **You keep control:** every step is your click. The few automatic sequences finish within about 1–3 s of their trigger.
* **Moderate, consistent level:** all effects sit within a narrow loudness band, well below speech. The root-cause reveal is deliberately about 3 dB more present.
* **Offline and safe:** all audio is embedded as standard WAV inside the .pptx; there are no links and no video. Every sound was synthesized from scratch for this deck, so there are no third-party samples and no licensing issues.
* Every slide's speaker note starts with **🔊 AUDIO** (sound on this slide) or **AUDIO: none (silent slide)**, so it is visible in Presenter View.

## The counters (slide 12)

`01 → 02 → 03 → 04 → 05 → 06` as split-flap digits, with a short mechanical tick on each change. The final **06** lands with a soft double beep as the confirmation.
Then one click each for **4** (times the main breaker tripped), **2** (sites on diesel generators) and **7** (days to trace the cause). All figures come from the report timeline.

## Before the meeting (3 minutes)

1. On the presentation laptop and room audio, run slides 11, 12 and 22 in Slide Show mode and set the room volume once.
2. If the sound is unsuitable on the day, open the SILENT file: it is click-for-click identical. Muting the laptop also works; the slides and clicks behave exactly the same.
