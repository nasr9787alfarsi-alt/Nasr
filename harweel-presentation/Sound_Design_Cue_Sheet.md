**Deliberate silence:** the callouts on slide 3, the deep-time labels, all three challenge texts, the step
cards on slides 10–11, the closing quote on slide 15 and the Thank You slide, so your voice carries
the key explanations.

**Levels:** set by loudness (RMS around −36 to −24 dBFS, peaks no higher than −6 dBFS), so they sit
under a speaking voice at normal room volume. PowerPoint has no volume control for animation sounds,
so do a sound check in the room. If the sounds are too loud, lower the laptop volume a few steps.

**To change or remove a sound:** in the Animation Pane, select the effect → Effect Options → Sound →
choose "[No Sound]" (or another WAV). For the slide 11 transition: Transitions tab → Sound.
