# Harweel Field — Animated Numbers (cue sheet)

File: `Harweel_Field_Enhanced_Animated_Numbers.pptx`, built from the approved
`Harweel_Field_Enhanced_Sound_Design.pptx` (kept unchanged as the backup). Only slides 4, 7, 12 and 14 differ;
the other 12 slides are byte-identical to the approved version.

Times are from the moment a slide's animations begin. Every counter is native and editable: a stack of text boxes
revealed in sequence on the same accelerate/decelerate curve PowerPoint uses for the matching needle or motion path.
The final value sits on top, so Normal view, print and PDF show the correct figure.

**Not animated, on purpose:** years on the timeline, 950×, 10 %, 3–5 km (schematic, not to scale). There is no
temperature data in the source, so no temperature counter.

**Accuracy:** the period dates and colours follow the International Chronostratigraphic Chart. The base of the
Cambrian is 538.8 Ma, so about 540 Ma sits on the Ediacaran–Cambrian boundary. The scan line through the block is
schematic: it does not assign ages to the individual rock layers above the salt.

**Sounds:** still never overlapping on any slide (checked against the real audio lengths), still quiet enough for
a live voice.
